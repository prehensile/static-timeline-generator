# Time-blindness watch

I am absolutely astounded that some (most?) people have pretty clear visualisations of time in their head: they know exactly where they are in the current week, month, year etc. For me? As I said at the time: “have you ever ridden a bike in freezing fog? It’s kind of like that. There’s this soup of upcoming things I know vaguely about, and they crystallise onto my eyebrows as I move towards them”.

I've built various things over the years to help with this, including [this webpage](https://prehensile.co.uk/clock/), which draws progress bars to visualise time at a few different scales. In 2024, I was able to bring the same idea to a cheap hackable smartwatch, using [Espruino](https://www.espruino.com/).

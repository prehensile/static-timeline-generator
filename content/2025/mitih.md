# MediaCity Immersive Technologies Innovation Hub
MITIH is an [Innovate UK funded](https://iuk-business-connect.org.uk/casestudy/transforming-immersive-technology-innovation-the-impact-of-mitih/) accelerator for creative technology innovation in Greater Manchester. I acted as their Head of Innovation from January - April 2025, running a programme matchmaking artists with technologists for an innovation showcase in early 2025, contributing to funding bids, and managing external relationships. I have returned to help with facilitation and planning on other strands of activity in 2025 and 2026.

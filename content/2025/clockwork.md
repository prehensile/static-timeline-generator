# Clockwork Dog: Audience tracking R&D
Clockwork Dog build hardware and software systems that run escape rooms and immersive interactive experiences. They came to me in May 2025 with an R&D brief: they wanted to add individual audience member tracking as an option in their builds. I researched and evaluated a few possible technologies for the job, then built a prototype system around our chosen technology to test it in action. I delivered a first working version of an integration with their showrunning system, and a healthy relationship with an excellent technology partner.

# Thrifty Ink

Thrifty Ink is a DIY printmaking project I started in 2023. I've always loved big, bold typographic prints and the idea of making them by hand, but the traditional typesetting process is very fiddly and time-intensive — as well as expensive, if you want to start buying your own type and equipment. So I designed a typesetting system that can be made out of laser-cut plywood, and printed with simple block-printing techniques.

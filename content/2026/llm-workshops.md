# Local LLM Workshops
I'm very interested in decentralised, community-level infrastructure for AI (and any significant technology, really), and the shifts in power structures and citizen agency that are possible when technical capability moves from large central players to communities and individuals.
To this end, I've been running workshops for people who are interested in running LLMs and AI services on their own computers. [The Worst Possible AI Server](#bad-ai) is another product of the same set of interests.

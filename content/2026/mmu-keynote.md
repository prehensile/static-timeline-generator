# High Tide: Some ways to think about the AI hype machine

I was invited to present a keynote speech at the Arts & Humanities AI Summit at Manchester Metropolitan University in June 2026.

I spoke about the need to speak and think about AI with nuance; acknowledging the usefulness that we're all getting from the technology, but also its significant negative effects in the world. I sketched out the way I think things will shake down over the next few years and encouraged the audience to hold on to the practices and tools they find helpful, while emphasising where we have agency to change the things we don't, and why I think human creativity is irreplaceable.
